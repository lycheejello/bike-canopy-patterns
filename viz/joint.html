STRUT JOINT — zoom (no zip ties)

stringer (½" PVC, LED rail)
LED strip — on top, facing cloth
strut — ⅜" rod, plugs into drilled holes
hoop tube (holds the cloth)
retaining screw at each socket
drag = orbit · scroll = zoom. The strut is a rod that plugs into a drilled hole — no coping, no clamp. The hole goes in the side of the stringer so it clears the LED on top. Slide the angle: keep it well onto the side. Same plug-in at the hoop end; lock with epoxy or a screw.
